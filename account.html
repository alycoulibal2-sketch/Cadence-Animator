Your account

Your email is your account.

It is created at checkout, when you buy Pro. There is nothing to register, no password to remember, and nothing to sign into to use the free app. Enter the email you paid with and your key to check it.

Check a licence key

Keys look like a group of letters and digits and are tied to the email used at checkout.

How Pro works once you have a key

  1. Pro is the same downloadThere is no separate Pro installer. Download Cadence like anyone else — or keep the copy you already have.
  2. Enter the key in the appOpen Settings → Pro, paste your key and the email you paid with. The Pro features switch on in that copy.
  3. Lost it?The key was shown on your thank-you page and is tied to your Stripe purchase. Write from the purchase email and it is re-sent; keep your Stripe receipt as proof.

Ten-line privacy policy: what a purchase records. Refunds: 14 days, no questions.
