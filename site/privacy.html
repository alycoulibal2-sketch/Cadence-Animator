Privacy

Ten lines. That is the whole policy.

Cadence is a desktop app that keeps your work on your PC. There is very little to say about your data, because there is very little of it.

  1. The app has no account, no sign-in and no telemetry. It never sends usage data, crash reports or identifiers anywhere.
  2. This website has no analytics, no cookies and no trackers. It loads one webfont from Google Fonts and asks the GitHub API for public numbers; nothing about you is sent to either.
  3. Your projects, autosaves, settings and a debug log live under %APPDATA%\Cadence Animator on your own machine, and nowhere else.
  4. When you load a rig, the app fetches meshes and textures from Roblox’s asset servers, and looks a username up on users.roblox.com only when you type one. Roblox sees those requests, as it would from Studio.
  5. The installed build asks GitHub for the release list to notice updates. GitHub sees a request for a public page. Nothing downloads or installs without your click.
  6. The phone companion is off by default. When you turn it on, a temporary Cloudflare tunnel carries your own viewport to your own phone, gated by a token; it ends when the app closes.
  7. If you buy Pro, Stripe records the email you enter and the payment, as any card payment does. Our licence server keeps that email and the key it issued so the key can be verified. That is the complete list.
  8. We never see or store your card details; Stripe handles the payment. We do not send marketing email. A receipt and your key are the only messages a purchase produces.
  9. To delete your licence record, write from the purchase email and it is removed; the key stops verifying at the same time. Refunds are 14 days, no questions.
  10. The source code is public, so every statement above can be checked against it. If you find it says otherwise, open an issue and it gets fixed in the open.

Last reviewed with Cadence 0.10.0. The safety page links each network and file claim to its source line.
