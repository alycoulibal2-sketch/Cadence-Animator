Four safety facts

What Cadence does, and doesn’t do.

  • No chat with strangers

    No messages, friends lists or feeds. Nobody can reach your child through Cadence.

  • No account needed to animate

    No sign-up, no email, no age. Your child opens it and makes.

  • Your words aren’t stored

    When Cadence AI launches, it reads what your child types to write the plan, then throws it away.

  • Moves come from a library of real Roblox clips

    Cadence AI will only arrange checked clips. It can’t chat, browse the web or make pictures.
