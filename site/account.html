How a key works

  1. Pro is the same downloadThere is no separate Pro installer. Download Cadence like anyone else — or keep the copy you already have.
  2. Enter the key in the appPress Ctrl K and run Cadence Pro: enter your key, then paste your key with the email you paid with. The Pro features switch on in that copy.
  3. Lost it?The key was shown on your thank-you page and isn’t emailed. Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the purchase email with your Stripe receipt, and it is re-sent.
Bought Pro before the new plans? You’re a Founder: Pro forever, nothing to renew. Your key keeps working.

What a key check sends, and what the licence server keeps (nothing): the ten-line privacy policy. Refunds: 14 days, no questions. Plans and Manage plan open with the new plans.
