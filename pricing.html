Questions

For parents

When can I buy a plan?

Not yet: payments open together with Cadence AI. Until then the animator is free and complete, and nothing here can charge you.

What will count toward the 3 a day?

Only new animations Cadence AI makes for you. Follow-up changes, plan edits and quick moves won’t be counted.

Does it need internet?

Cadence AI will: it runs on Corvexsa’s computer, so there’s nothing to download. Quick moves, follow-ups and building by hand will work without it — and the animator works offline today.

What do I own?

Every animation you make is yours to use in your games, free or paid. Your projects are saved on your PC, not online.

Can I cancel?

When plans open: anytime, from Manage plan. Pro keeps working until the time you paid for ends. Full refund within 14 days.

I bought the old Pro. What happens?

You’re a Founder: Pro forever, including everything new that Pro gets. There’s nothing to do — your key keeps working.
